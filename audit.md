# Evidence audit

One row per claim in the phase 1 summary (and the phase 2 context paragraph), with the status it had after
phase 1 and the status after the phase 2 captures. Observed means a value in `results\<scenario>.json` (steps are
addressed by name: `Steps[connect-exo]` is the step whose `Name` is `connect-exo`) or a line in the matching
transcript. Inferred names what it rests on. Nothing was upgraded without a capture and nothing was downgraded
silently; rows whose capture cannot be made say so.

(child script 2.0 for the phase 2 re-run of graph-connect-then-exo; 1.0 or 1.1 for the other phase 1 runs),
ExchangeOnlineManagement 3.10.1.
